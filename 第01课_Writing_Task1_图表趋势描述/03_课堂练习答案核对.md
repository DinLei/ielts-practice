## Ex.11 副词分类

Big: significantly, considerably, **substantially, dramatically** ✅
Small: slightly, **moderately** ✅
Gradual: steadily, **gradually, consistently** ✅

⚠️ 你把 **slowly** 放在了 Small change。slowly 描述的是**速度**不是幅度，更合适放在 Gradual change。不算大错，但要分清：slightly = 幅度小；slowly = 速度慢。

## Ex.14 / 15 配图与填空

1 Dubai → 图 B ｜ 2 Hong Kong → 图 C ｜ 3 London → 图 A ✅

老师红笔圈的 **by** 和 **to** 是这页最重要的点：
- plummeted **by** just over 40% → 跌了 40 个点
- went up again rapidly in 2010 **to** approximately £600,000 → 涨到 60 万

London 那句你改了两次（dropped → decreased substantially），两个都可以，"fell by almost £100,000" 最简洁。
