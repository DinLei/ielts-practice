## 二、逐句批改

### P1 Introduction

> The bar chart gives information about the changes in proportion of households who owned or rented homes in England and Wales from 1918 to 2011.

✅ 改写思路对：用了 *gives information about*，把 *in owned and rented accommodation* 改成了从句。

| 问题 | 改法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| the changes in proportion | changes in **the** proportion | proportion 是可数名词，前面要有冠词 |
| households **who** | households **that** | household 是"户"不是人，用 that 更准 |
| rented homes | rented **their** homes | own / rent one's home 是固定说法 |

✅ **改后**：The bar chart gives information about changes in the proportion of households in England and Wales that owned or rented their homes between 1918 and 2011.

### P2 Overview

> Overall, there was a steady shift of poluation from renting accommodation to buying their own house.

- ❌ 拼写：poluation → **population**。
- ❌ **借来的句子没改到位**：Case 1 写的是人口从农村搬到城市，所以说 *shift of the population*。这道题测的是**家庭的住房方式**，不是人口迁移。
- ❌ **buying ≠ owning**：图上是"拥有"住房的比例，不是"购买"住房的比例。
- ❌ their 指代不明：前面只有 population（单数），没有复数名词可以指。

✅ Overall, there was a steady shift **from renting to home ownership** over the period.

> People renting houses made up the vast majority at the start of the period, but they had been outnumbered by households owning accommodation by the end, with the turning point coming in 1970s.

👍 这句骨架很好：made up the vast majority / had been outnumbered by … by the end / with the turning point coming …，过去完成时和独立主格都用对了。

- ⚠️ **People** renting **houses** → **Households** renting **their homes**。图上的单位是"户"不是"人"；租的也不一定是 house（独栋房），可能是公寓，用 homes 或 accommodation。
- ❌ in 1970s → in **the** 1970s（年代前要加 the）。不过这道题交叉点正好落在 1971 年，写 **around 1971** 更准确。

✅ Households renting their homes made up the vast majority at the start of the period, but they had been outnumbered by owner-occupiers by the end, with the turning point coming around 1971.

### P3 Body 1

> In 1918, only almost 22% of people owned their own houses, compared with 78% renting houses.

- ❌ **数据错**：图上 1918 年是 **23%**（自有）和 **77%**（租赁），不是 22 和 78。
- ❌ **only almost 自相矛盾**：only 表示"才这么少"，almost 表示"差一点就到"，两个放一起意思打架。和 #011 的 substantial dip 是同一类错。读到的数是 23，就写 *only 23%*；拿不准就写 *just under a quarter*。
- ⚠️ people → households（同上）。

✅ In 1918, only 23% of households owned their homes, compared with 77% that rented.

> Over the following 100 years, the share of renting houses fell steadily to nearly 36% in 2011 while the proportion of owning houses increased by roughly 40% to 62%.

这句有 5 个问题：

| 问题 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| following **100 years** | 1918 到 2011 是 **93 年**，不能凑整成 100 |
| the share of **renting houses** | "正在租房的房子"，主语错了。应为 the share of **households renting** / **renting** |
| fell **steadily** to 36% | **不准**：租房先降到 2001 年的 **31%**，2011 年又**回升**到 36%，不是一路稳步下降 |
| increased **by roughly 40%** | 两个百分比相减要说 **percentage points**（错题集 #008）；23 → 64 是 **41 个百分点** |
| **62%** | 图上 2011 年是 **64%** |

**结构问题更大**：这一句把 1918–2011 全程都写完了，下一段又从 1971 写到 2011，**同一段时间写了两遍**。中间 1939–1953 保持在 32%、1961 升到 41% 这些特征反而没写。04 作业里规划的是 Body 1 写 **1918–1971**，Body 2 写 **1971–2011**，按这个分。

✅ Ownership rose to 32% in 1939 and remained unchanged until 1953, before climbing to 41% in 1961.

### P4 Body 2

> The two figures crossed in 1971, both of them accounted 50% and in the next 4 decades the proportion of households owning accommodation reached a peak in 2001 at nearly 70%, then it fell slightly to roughly 62% in 2011, but still twice as many as the proportion of renting houses.

这一句太长了，连了 4 个分句。拆开看：

| 片段 | 问题 | 改法 |
|---|---|---|
| The two figures **crossed** in 1971 | 1971 年两者**正好相等**（50/50），还没有交叉。交叉发生在 1971 之后 | By 1971, the two figures **had converged** / **were equal** |
| , **both of them accounted 50%** | ① 逗号直接连两个完整句子（comma splice）；② account 后面要加 **for** | , **with each accounting for** half of all households |
| in the next **4** decades … reached a peak **in 2001** | 1971 到 2001 是 3 个十年，不是 4 个；正式写作里数字 1–10 一般用英文单词拼写 | **Over the next three decades** |
| reached a peak in 2001 at nearly 70% | 能看懂，语序不自然 | reached a peak **of 69% in 2001** |
| then it fell slightly | then 不能连接两个句子 | 断句，或写成 …, **before falling** slightly |
| to roughly **62%** | 图上是 **64%** | to 64% |
| **but still twice as many as** the proportion | ① 缺主语和谓语（but **it was** still …）；② proportion 比较用 **high**，不能用 many；③ 64 / 36 ≈ 1.8，不到两倍，要加 **almost** | but it was still **almost twice as high as** the figure for renting |

还漏了一个关键特征：**租房比例在 2001 年降到最低点 31%**。

✅ 拆成三句：

By 1971, the two figures had converged, with each accounting for half of all households. Over the next three decades, home ownership continued to rise, reaching a peak of 69% in 2001, while renting fell to a low of 31%. In the final decade, however, this trend was partially reversed: ownership fell slightly to 64% in 2011, but it was still almost twice as high as the figure for renting (36%).

## 三、四项评分

| 评分项 | 分数 | 得分点 | 扣分点 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Task Achievement** | 6 | 有清楚的 Overview；1971 转折点、2001 峰值、2011 回落都提到了 | 4 处数据和图不符（22 / 62 ×2 / 100 年 / fell steadily）；漏了 1939–1953 不变、2001 租房最低点 |
| **Coherence & Cohesion** | 6 | 四段结构清楚；Overview 的位置和写法都对 | 两个 Body 时间重叠；最后一句塞了 4 个分句；both of them / then it 衔接生硬 |
| **Lexical Resource** | 6 | 用上了 steady shift、vast majority、outnumbered、turning point 等高分表达 | 拼写 poluation；renting houses 作类别名；people 和 households 混用；twice as many as 修饰 proportion |
| **Grammatical Range & Accuracy** | 6 | 用了过去完成时被动、独立主格、compared with；时态全对 | accounted 漏 for；comma splice；but 后缺主谓；in 1970s 缺 the |

**要上 6.5–7，下一篇只需要改三件事：**
1. **每个数字写下前，回图上核对一遍。**
2. **按时间切段，两段不重叠**：先在草稿上写好 Body 1 和 Body 2 各自覆盖哪几年。
3. **一句最多两个分句**，超过就断句。
