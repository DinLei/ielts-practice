# Task 1 图表题：解题思路与句式库

写作业时把这个文件放在旁边。句式里的 `___` 是让你填的位置。

---

## 一、考场流程（20 分钟）

| 时间 | 做什么 |
|---|---|
| 0–3 min | **读图**：标题、两个轴、单位、时间范围、图例。圈出最大、最小、交叉点、转折点 |
| 3–5 min | **规划**：Overview 写哪 2 个特征？Body 怎么分两组？ |
| 5–18 min | **写**：4 段，150–180 词。不要超过 200 词，没时间检查 |
| 18–20 min | **检查**：数字对不对、时态、单复数、by / to |

不写规划直接动笔，是写不出来的主要原因。先用 1–2 分钟把 4 段各写什么用中文列出来。

---

## 二、四段结构（固定）

| 段 | 句数 | 写什么 | 不写什么 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Introduction | 1 句 | 改写题目：图表类型 + 内容 + 地点 + 时间 | 不抄题 |
| 2 Overview | 2 句 | 最明显的 2–3 个总体特征 | **不写具体数字** |
| 3 Body 1 | 3–4 句 | 第一组数据，带数字 | 不解释原因 |
| 4 Body 2 | 3–4 句 | 第二组数据，带数字，做比较 | 不写结论、不写观点 |

不需要 conclusion。Overview 就是 Task 1 的"结论"，放在前面写。

**Body 怎么分组**（三选一）：
- 按**时间段**：前半段 / 后半段（有明显转折点时用，作业就是这种）
- 按**类别**：A 项 / B 项（两条线各写一段）
- 按**趋势**：上升的 / 下降的 / 不变的

---

## 三、Introduction：改写题目

题目通常是一句话，你要换说法但意思不变。四处可换：

| 原文 | 替换 |
|---|---|
| The chart **shows** | The bar chart **illustrates / compares / gives information about / provides data on** |
| **the percentage of** | **the proportion of** / **how many … in percentage terms** |
| **between 1918 and 2011** | **from 1918 to 2011** / **over the period 1918–2011** / **over a period of almost a century** |
| households **in owned accommodation** | households **that owned their homes** / **home-owning households** / **owner-occupied households** |

作业题改写示例：
> The bar chart **compares the proportions of** households **that owned and rented their homes** in England and Wales **over the period from 1918 to 2011**.

句式：
- The ___ (chart type) ___ (shows / compares / illustrates) ___ (what) in ___ (where) ___ (when).
- The ___ gives information about ___ over a period of ___ years.

---

## 四、Overview 句式

开头词：**Overall, …** / **It is clear that …** / **Generally speaking, …** / **The most striking feature is that …**

- Overall, ___ rose steadily over the period, while ___ declined.
- Overall, the two trends were almost mirror images of each other.
- It is clear that ___ was far more common at the start of the period, whereas ___ dominated by the end.
- ___ overtook ___ in ___ (year), and the gap between them widened thereafter.
- Although ___ increased overall, the rise was not continuous.

Overview 里不写数字，但可以写**比例关系**：roughly half、the majority、a minority、more than twice as many、the two figures were equal。

## 五、Body 句式库

### A. 动词 + 副词（主语是数据）

- ___ rose / increased / climbed **sharply** from ___ to ___ between ___ and ___.
- ___ fell / dropped / declined **by** ___% **to** ___ in ___.
- ___ remained **stable / unchanged** at around ___ from ___ to ___.
- ___ fluctuated between ___ and ___ during the ___s.
- ___ peaked at ___ in ___. / ___ reached a peak of ___ in ___.
- ___ hit a low of ___ in ___.
- ___ grew steadily, reaching ___ by ___.

### B. There was + 形容词 + 名词

- There was a **dramatic / steady / slight** increase in ___ between ___ and ___.
- There was a drop of ___% in ___ over the following decade.
- This was followed by a **gradual** decline in ___.

### C. 时间段作主语（高级一点）

- The period from ___ to ___ saw a reversal of this trend.
- The following two decades witnessed a steady rise in ___.
- The years between ___ and ___ were marked by little change.

### D. 起点、终点、某一点的值

- In ___, ___ stood at ___. / ___ accounted for ___ of households. / ___ made up ___ of the total.
- Starting at ___ in ___, ___ then …
- **By** ___, ___ **had** risen / fallen **to** ___.（by + 过去完成时）
- At the end of the period, ___ was ___.

### E. 比较 / 对比

- ___ was **twice as high as** ___. / ___ was **three times higher than** ___.
- ___, **whereas / while** ___.
- **In contrast,** … / **By comparison,** … / **Conversely,** …
- ___ **overtook / exceeded** ___ in ___ (year).
- The two figures **were equal** at ___ in ___. / The two lines **converged / crossed** in ___.
- The gap between ___ and ___ **widened / narrowed**.
- ___ was **the only** category to ___.

### F. 约数（图上读不准必须用）

approximately / roughly / around / about ___
just over / just under / slightly more than / slightly less than / nearly / almost / close to ___
a quarter / a third / half / two-thirds / three-quarters
the majority of / the minority of / the vast majority of

### G. 时间表达

in 1918 / between 1918 and 1939 / from 1939 to 1953
over the next two decades / during the 1970s / in the following decade
over the period / throughout the period / at the beginning (end) of the period
thereafter / subsequently / after that / from then on / by 2011

### H. 连接句子（避免每句都 "X rose. Y fell."）

- ___, **before** rising again to ___.
- ___, **after which** it remained stable.
- ___, **with** ___ **accounting for** ___.（with + 名词 + -ing）
- **Having** peaked in 2001, ___ then fell back slightly.
- ___ rose to ___, **a figure that** remained unchanged until ___.

## 六、介词速查（必背）

| 想表达 | 用 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| 变了多少 | **by** | rose **by** 10 percentage points |
| 变到多少 | **to** | rose **to** 60% |
| 从多少到多少 | **from … to …** | fell **from** 77% **to** 50% |
| 停在多少 | **at** | remained stable **at** 32% |
| 名词 + 幅度 | **of** | an increase **of** 9% |
| 名词 + 对象 | **in** | an increase **in** home ownership |
| 某一年 | **in** | **in** 1971 |
| 一段时间 | **between … and … / over / during** | **over** the next decade |

百分比的变化，严格说是 **percentage points**（50% 到 60% 是 10 个百分点），考试里写 "by 10%" 也不扣分，但知道 "percentage points" 更准确。

## 七、Task 1 常见扣分点

- [ ] 抄题目原句 → 必须改写
- [ ] 没有 Overview，或者 Overview 里塞满数字
- [ ] 解释原因（because / due to / as a result of） → 不要
- [ ] 时态乱：一段过去式一段现在式
- [ ] **increase up** ✗ / **decrease down** ✗（动词本身已有方向）
- [ ] **the percentage was increased** ✗ → 主动：**the percentage increased**
- [ ] **the number of** 只用于可数名词；百分比用 **the proportion / percentage of**
- [ ] 每一句都是 "X rose. Y fell." 的简单句 → 用第五部分 H 的连接方式合并
- [ ] 编数据：图上读不准就写 approximately / just under
- [ ] 字数不够 150 → 直接扣分；超过 200 → 没时间检查，错误变多
