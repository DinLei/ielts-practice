The bar chart compares the proportions of households that owned and rented their homes in England and Wales at nine points between 1918 and 2011.

Overall, home ownership rose steadily over the period while renting declined, and the two trends were almost mirror images of each other. Although rented accommodation was far more common at the start of the period, owner-occupied households formed the majority from the 1970s onwards.

In 1918, only 23% of households owned their homes, whereas 77% rented. Ownership rose to roughly a third by 1939 and remained unchanged in 1953, before climbing to just over 40% in 1961. By 1971 the two figures had converged, with each accounting for exactly half of all households.

From then on, the pattern reversed. The proportion of home owners continued to grow, reaching 60% in 1981 and peaking at around 69% in 2001, while renting fell to a low of 31% in the same year. However, this trend was partially reversed in the final decade: by 2011, ownership had dropped slightly to about 64%, and the share of rented households had risen again to 36%.

## 逐段注释

### Para 1
- `compares the proportions of households that owned and rented` ← 题目的 "the percentage of households in owned and rented accommodation" 改写：percentage → proportions；"in owned accommodation" → 定语从句 "that owned their homes"
- `at nine points between 1918 and 2011` ← 比 "between 1918 and 2011" 多一点信息（9 个年份），显示你读图了

### Para 2 Overview
- 句 1 三个信息：owned 升、rented 降、镜像关系。`while` 做对比。
- 句 2 用 `Although … , …` 让步句，对比开头和结尾谁是多数。`from the 1970s onwards` 暗示 1971 交叉点，但不写数字。
- 两句都没有具体数字，但有 "majority" 这种比例词。

### Para 3 Body 1
- 句 1 `only 23% … whereas 77%` ← 一句话放两个数字做对比，`only` 带出"少"的评价（这是允许的，不算观点）
- 句 2 三个时间点压进一句：`rose to … by 1939` / `remained unchanged in 1953` / `before climbing to … in 1961`。`before + -ing` 把下一个动作接上，避免再起一句。`roughly a third` ← 32% 的约数写法。
- 句 3 `By 1971 … had converged` ← by + 过去完成时；`with each accounting for exactly half` ← with + 名词 + -ing 补充数据。

### Para 4 Body 2
- 句 1 一句话过渡：`From then on, the pattern reversed.` 短句也可以，节奏变化。
- 句 2 `continued to grow, reaching … and peaking at …` ← 分词短语连续接两个数据点；`while renting fell to a low of` ← 同年对比。
- 句 3 `However` 引出反转；冒号后用两个 `had + 过去分词` 并列。`the share of rented households` ← 换了说法，不再重复 "the proportion of"。

### 用到的句式（对照 02 句式库）
| 句式 | 范文里的位置 |
|---|---|
| X rose to … by … | Ownership rose to roughly a third by 1939 |
| remained unchanged | remained unchanged in 1953 |
| before + -ing | before climbing to just over 40% |
| By + year + had + pp | By 1971 the two figures had converged |
| with + noun + -ing | with each accounting for exactly half |
| reaching … / peaking at … | reaching 60% in 1981 and peaking at around 69% |
| fell to a low of | renting fell to a low of 31% |
| whereas / while | 各出现一次 |
| 约数 | roughly a third / just over 40% / around 69% / about 64% |

### 换一种分组的 Overview + Body 开头（按类别分）
如果按 owned / rented 分段，Body 1 可以这样起头：
> The proportion of owner-occupied households grew almost continuously over the period. Starting at just 23% in 1918, it reached a third by 1939 and half by 1971, and went on to peak at 69% in 2001 before slipping back to 64% in 2011.

Body 2 对应写 rented，用 `Conversely, …` 开头。
