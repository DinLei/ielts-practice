# 背诵范文：折线图 三国国际游客 2000–2018

## P1 Introduction

The line graph compares the number of international tourists visiting Thailand, Egypt and Vietnam over an 18-year period from 2000 to 2018.
折线图比较了 2000 到 2018 这 18 年间到访泰国、埃及和越南的国际游客人数。

## P2 Overview

Overall, Thailand attracted by far the most visitors throughout the period and saw the largest growth, while Vietnam experienced the most rapid rise in proportional terms.
总体上，泰国全程游客遥遥领先且增量最大，而越南按比例涨得最快。
Egypt was the only country whose figures fluctuated, and by the end of the period it had been overtaken by Vietnam.
埃及是唯一数字出现波动的国家，到期末已被越南超过。

## P3 Body 1

In 2000, Thailand received around 10 million tourists, roughly twice as many as Egypt (5 million) and five times the figure for Vietnam (2 million).
2000 年泰国接待约 1000 万游客，大约是埃及（500 万）的两倍、越南（200 万）的五倍。
Thai arrivals grew modestly to 14 million by 2009, and then accelerated sharply, reaching 38 million in 2018.
泰国的到访人数到 2009 年小幅增至 1400 万，之后急剧加速，2018 年达到 3800 万。
Vietnam followed a similar pattern on a smaller scale: numbers remained below 4 million until 2009 before climbing steeply to 15 million in 2018.
越南模式相似但规模较小：人数在 2009 年之前一直低于 400 万，之后陡升至 2018 年的 1500 万。

## P4 Body 2

Egypt, by contrast, followed a very different trajectory.
埃及则走出了完全不同的轨迹。
Visitor numbers rose steadily from 5 million to a peak of 12 million in 2009, but then declined to a low of 9 million in 2015.
游客数从 500 万稳步升至 2009 年的峰值 1200 万，随后跌至 2015 年的低点 900 万。
Although there was a partial recovery to 11 million in 2018, this was still below the figure recorded nine years earlier, and 4 million lower than that of Vietnam.
尽管 2018 年部分回升到 1100 万，但仍低于九年前的数字，并且比越南少 400 万。

# 汉译英：住房自有与租赁 1918–2011

In 1918, only 23% of households owned their homes.
1918 年只有 23% 的家庭拥有自己的住房。
The proportion of owner-occupied households peaked at around 69% in 2001.
自有住房比例在 2001 年达到峰值，约为 69%。
The proportion of households renting fell from 77% to 50%.
租房家庭的比例从 77% 下降到 50%。
By 1971, the two figures had converged.
到 1971 年，两个数字已经持平。
The proportion remained unchanged between 1939 and 1953.
1939 年到 1953 年之间，这一比例保持不变。
House prices fell by almost £100,000 in 2009, but went up again rapidly the following year.
房价在 2009 年下跌了将近 10 万英镑，但次年又迅速回升。
Overall, home ownership rose steadily, while renting declined.
总体来看，自有住房稳步上升，而租房则下降。
This trend was partially reversed in the final decade.
这一趋势在最后十年里部分逆转了。
The proportion of home owners was twice as high as that of renters.
自有住房比例是租房的两倍。
In 2011, the share of rented households rose slightly to 36%.
2011 年，租房家庭的比例小幅回升到 36%。
