# 背诵范文：折线图 三国国际游客 2000–2018

## P1 Introduction

The line graph compares the number of international tourists visiting Thailand, Egypt and Vietnam over an 18-year period from 2000 to 2018.
折线图比较了 2000 到 2018 这 18 年间到访泰国、埃及和越南的国际游客人数。

## P2 Overview

Overall, Thailand attracted by far the most visitors throughout the period and saw the largest growth, while Vietnam experienced the most rapid rise in proportional terms.
总体上，泰国全程游客遥遥领先且增量最大，而越南按比例涨得最快。
Egypt was the only country whose figures fluctuated, and by the end of the period it had been overtaken by Vietnam.
埃及是唯一数字出现波动的国家，到期末已被越南超过。

## P3 Body 1

In 2000, Thailand received around 10 million tourists, roughly twice as many as Egypt (5 million) and five times the figure for Vietnam (2 million).
2000 年泰国接待约 1000 万游客，大约是埃及（500 万）的两倍、越南（200 万）的五倍。
Thai arrivals grew modestly to 14 million by 2009, and then accelerated sharply, reaching 38 million in 2018.
泰国的到访人数到 2009 年小幅增至 1400 万，之后急剧加速，2018 年达到 3800 万。
Vietnam followed a similar pattern on a smaller scale: numbers remained below 4 million until 2009 before climbing steeply to 15 million in 2018.
越南模式相似但规模较小：人数在 2009 年之前一直低于 400 万，之后陡升至 2018 年的 1500 万。

## P4 Body 2

Egypt, by contrast, followed a very different trajectory.
埃及则走出了完全不同的轨迹。
Visitor numbers rose steadily from 5 million to a peak of 12 million in 2009, but then declined to a low of 9 million in 2015.
游客数从 500 万稳步升至 2009 年的峰值 1200 万，随后跌至 2015 年的低点 900 万。
Although there was a partial recovery to 11 million in 2018, this was still below the figure recorded nine years earlier, and 4 million lower than that of Vietnam.
尽管 2018 年部分回升到 1100 万，但仍低于九年前的数字，并且比越南少 400 万。

# 背诵范文：柱状图 城乡人口 1950–2020

## P1 Introduction

The bar chart compares the proportions of people living in urban and rural areas of Country X over a 70-year period from 1950 to 2020.
柱状图比较了 X 国 1950 到 2020 这 70 年间城市和农村人口所占的比例。

## P2 Overview

Overall, there was a steady shift of the population from the countryside to towns and cities.
总体上，人口持续从农村向城镇迁移。
Rural residents made up the vast majority at the start of the period, but they had been outnumbered by urban dwellers by the end, with the turning point coming in the 1990s.
期初农村居民占绝大多数，但到期末已被城市居民超过，转折点出现在 90 年代。

## P3 Body 1

In 1950, only 18% of the population lived in urban areas, compared with 82% in rural areas.
1950 年只有 18% 的人口住在城市，农村则是 82%。
Over the next four decades, the urban share rose steadily by roughly seven or eight percentage points every ten years, reaching 48% in 1990, while the rural figure fell by the same amount to 52%.
此后四十年，城市比例每十年稳步上升约七八个百分点，1990 年达到 48%，农村比例则以同样幅度降到 52%。

## P4 Body 2

Some time between 1990 and 2000, the two figures crossed, and urban residents overtook rural residents for the first time, accounting for 55% of the population in 2000.
1990 到 2000 年之间的某个时候，两个数字发生交叉，城市居民首次超过农村居民，2000 年占人口的 55%。
This trend continued over the following two decades.
这一趋势在随后二十年里延续。
By 2020, the proportion of people living in cities had climbed to 71%, almost two and a half times the rural figure of 29%.
到 2020 年，城市人口比例已升至 71%，几乎是农村 29% 的两倍半。

# 背诵范文：带预测折线图 电动车销售占比 2015–2035

## P1 Introduction

The line graph shows the percentage of new cars sold that were electric in three countries between 2015 and 2025, along with projections up to 2035.
折线图展示了 2015 到 2025 年三个国家新车销量中电动车所占的比例，以及到 2035 年的预测。

## P2 Overview

Overall, electric cars have gained market share in all three countries, and this trend is expected to continue.
总体上，电动车在三个国家的市场份额都在上升，而且这一趋势预计会持续。
Country A has led throughout and is predicted to approach 100%, while the most dramatic growth in the coming decade is forecast for Countries B and C.
A 国一直领先，预计将接近 100%；而未来十年增长最剧烈的预计是 B 国和 C 国。

## P3 Body 1

In 2015, electric vehicles made up 20% of new car sales in Country A, but only 2% and 1% in Countries B and C respectively.
2015 年，电动车占 A 国新车销量的 20%，而在 B 国和 C 国分别只有 2% 和 1%。
Over the next ten years, the figure for Country A rose sharply to 85%, whereas Country B saw a more modest increase to 20%, and Country C remained below 5%.
此后十年，A 国的数字急升到 85%，B 国的增幅较小，升到 20%，C 国则一直低于 5%。

## P4 Body 2

Looking ahead, Country A's share is projected to level off at around 98% by 2035.
展望未来，A 国的份额预计到 2035 年趋于平稳，约为 98%。

相比之下，B 国预计涨幅最大，增长三倍多，达到 70%。
Country C is also forecast to grow rapidly, reaching 40%, although it will still lag well behind the other two countries.

# 背诵范文：作业改后全文 住房自有与租赁

## P1 Introduction

The bar chart gives information about changes in the proportion of households in England and Wales that owned or rented their homes between 1918 and 2011.
柱状图展示了 1918 到 2011 年英格兰和威尔士拥有住房和租房的家庭比例的变化。

## P2 Overview

Overall, there was a steady shift from renting to home ownership over the period.
总体上，这一期间出现了从租房向自有住房的稳步转变。
Households renting their homes made up the vast majority at the start of the period, but they had been outnumbered by owner-occupiers by the end, with the turning point coming around 1971.
期初租房家庭占绝大多数，但到期末已被自有住房家庭超过，转折点出现在 1971 年前后。

## P3 Body 1

In 1918, only 23% of households owned their homes, compared with 77% that rented.
1918 年只有 23% 的家庭拥有自己的住房，租房的则有 77%。
Ownership rose to 32% in 1939 and remained unchanged until 1953, before climbing to 41% in 1961.
自有比例在 1939 年升到 32%，一直保持到 1953 年不变，之后在 1961 年攀升到 41%。
By 1971, the two figures had converged, with each accounting for half of all households.
到 1971 年，两个数字已经持平，各占全部家庭的一半。

## P4 Body 2

Over the next three decades, home ownership continued to rise, reaching a peak of 69% in 2001, while renting fell to a low of 31%.
此后三十年，自有比例继续上升，2001 年达到峰值 69%，而租房比例降到最低的 31%。
In the final decade, however, this trend was partially reversed: ownership fell slightly to 64% in 2011, but it was still almost twice as high as the figure for renting (36%).
然而最后十年这一趋势部分逆转：自有比例在 2011 年小幅回落到 64%，但仍几乎是租房比例（36%）的两倍。
