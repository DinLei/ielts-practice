## 二、自测一：汉译英（写在横线上，答案在下方）

1. 1918 年只有 23% 的家庭拥有自己的住房。
   ______________________________________________
2. 自有住房比例在 2001 年达到峰值，约为 69%。
   ______________________________________________
3. 租房家庭的比例从 77% 下降到 50%。
   ______________________________________________
4. 到 1971 年，两个数字已经持平。
   ______________________________________________
5. 1939 年到 1953 年之间，这一比例保持不变。
   ______________________________________________
6. 房价在 2009 年下跌了将近 10 万英镑，但次年又迅速回升。
   ______________________________________________
7. 总体来看，自有住房稳步上升，而租房则下降。
   ______________________________________________
8. 这一趋势在最后十年里部分逆转了。
   ______________________________________________
9. 自有住房比例是租房的两倍。
   ______________________________________________
10. 2011 年，租房家庭的比例小幅回升到 36%。
   ______________________________________________

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

1. In 1918, only 23% of households owned their homes.
2. The proportion of owner-occupied households peaked at around 69% in 2001.
3. The proportion of households renting fell from 77% to 50%.
4. By 1971, the two figures had converged. / By 1971, the two figures were equal.
5. The proportion remained unchanged between 1939 and 1953.
6. House prices fell by almost £100,000 in 2009, but went up again rapidly the following year.
7. Overall, home ownership rose steadily, while renting declined.
8. This trend was partially reversed in the final decade.
9. The proportion of home owners was twice as high as that of renters.
10. In 2011, the share of rented households rose slightly to 36%.

</details>

## 三、自测二：句型转换（动词+副词 ↔ there was + 形容词 + 名词）

1. House prices rose dramatically. → There was ______________________.
2. There was a slight decrease in rental costs. → Rental costs ______________________.
3. The number of visitors fluctuated considerably. → There was ______________________.
4. There was a steady increase in home ownership. → Home ownership ______________________.
5. Sales plummeted. → There was ______________________.
6. There was a modest rise in interest rates. → Interest rates ______________________.

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

1. There was a dramatic rise in house prices.
2. Rental costs decreased slightly.
3. There was a considerable fluctuation in the number of visitors.
4. Home ownership increased steadily.
5. There was a dramatic fall / plunge in sales.
6. Interest rates rose modestly.

</details>

## 四、自测三：介词填空（by / to / at / from / of / in）

2. Renting fell ____ 10 percentage points over the decade.
3. The figure remained stable ____ around 32%.
4. There was an increase ____ 9% ____ home ownership.
